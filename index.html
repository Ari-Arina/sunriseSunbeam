Ariana's Beach Trip

Day 1: Shopping & the Pool

Many things were done that day!

  1. Explored Napel's Downtown
  2. Got to the AirBnB
  3. Organized all my stuff
  4. Started a shopping list for our vacation
  5. Went to Wallmart and got distracted by a cute duck plushie
  6. Went back to AirBnB and enjoyed the pool :D

Day 2: Beach in Englewood (rain & chaos)

  • Realized it rained all night
  • Got prepared to go to the beach as soon as it got sunny
  • Rained the moment we arrive to the beach

Although heartbroken, we went back to the AirBnB and found our passion in Monopoly! Playing until 2 PM to try to go back to the beach

This time back, the sky was cloudy and the waves were insane! Really recommend this beach if you love the thrill of the waves (do be careful!)

Englewood Beach

Day3: Siesta Beach & Relax

With a sunny sky, we went Siesta Beach. This was the most relaxing beach I ever been to!

  • Soft sand that never got too hot
  • Soft waves and cool waters
  • Shallow water
  • Amenities & rentals

10/10 check it out if it sounds like your vibe!

Siesta Beach

Day 4: Rest & Monopoly

Finally a day dedicated to the pool at the AirBnB!

We had pancakes and pizzas, while playing a 2 loong games of Monopoly! Did I go bankrupt multiple times? Yes! Did my dad survive a whole round with $2? Yes! (don't ask me how)

After, we packed up and got ready to leave the AirBnB the next day.

Day 5: Lagoon & Home

We finished cleaning out the house and said our goodbyes!

Arriving at Metro Water Lagoon was amazing! There were many things available:

  • Cabana
  • Water park
  • Slide
  • Endless pool
  • Kayacking

Although a storm did come and took out about 2 hours of our day, we had so much fun with nice food and lovely pool!

And thus, we go home.
